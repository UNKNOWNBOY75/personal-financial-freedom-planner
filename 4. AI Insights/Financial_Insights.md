## 4. SCENARIO & RISK ANALYSIS

### Scenario Comparison

The model uses three annual-return scenarios:

| Scenario | Assumed Return | Projected Portfolio at Age 45 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 8% | ₹1,38,43,916.71 |
| Base | 10% | ₹1,87,85,941.29 |
| Optimistic | 12% | ₹2,57,00,103.05 |

### AI-Generated Insight

The scenario analysis shows that the projected portfolio varies substantially when the assumed annual return changes.

The difference between the 8% and 12% scenarios illustrates the impact that long-term return assumptions can have on a 25-year projection.

The model should therefore be viewed as a scenario-planning tool rather than a precise forecast.

### Key Risks & Assumptions

1. Investment returns are assumed to remain constant within each scenario.
2. Actual market returns can fluctuate significantly.
3. Monthly investment contributions are assumed to remain constant at ₹15,000.
4. Income growth and inflation are not dynamically incorporated into the portfolio projection.
5. Future expenses may differ from the current ₹6,00,000 annual target.
6. The 4% withdrawal-rate assumption may not be appropriate for every situation.
7. Taxes, investment fees, and unexpected expenses are not included in the current model.

### Analytical Conclusion

The model demonstrates how savings behaviour, regular investment contributions, time, and assumed returns interact to influence long-term portfolio growth. The results should be updated whenever the underlying financial assumptions change.

## 5. AI-ASSISTED ANALYSIS WORKFLOW

### Role of AI

AI was used as an analytical support tool to interpret the outputs of the Excel financial model and convert numerical results into structured financial insights.

AI did not independently determine the user's financial inputs or guarantee future outcomes.

### AI Workflow

1. Financial data was entered and organized in Microsoft Excel.
2. Excel formulas calculated income, expenses, savings, investments, net worth, portfolio projections, and scenario results.
3. Key numerical outputs were extracted from the Excel model.
4. AI was provided with these outputs and relevant assumptions.
5. AI identified patterns, relationships, and important observations.
6. AI-generated observations were reviewed against the original Excel calculations.
7. Verified insights were documented in this file.
8. Limitations and assumptions were explicitly included to reduce misleading interpretations.

### Example AI Tasks

AI was used to:

- Interpret savings and spending patterns.
- Explain the effect of investment contributions and compounding.
- Compare conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios.
- Explain the relationship between the 4% rule and the required portfolio.
- Identify important model assumptions and limitations.
- Convert numerical outputs into clear, human-readable insights.

### Human Verification

All numerical conclusions were checked against the underlying Excel model before being included in the final analysis.

AI-generated content was treated as an analytical aid rather than an authoritative financial recommendation.

### Responsible AI Principles

The project follows these principles:

- Use AI to support analysis, not replace human judgment.
- Verify AI-generated numerical claims against source data.
- Clearly communicate assumptions and limitations.
- Avoid presenting projections as guaranteed outcomes.
- Protect personal financial information.
